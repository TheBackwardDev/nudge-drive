# Delete your Nudge Drive account

This page explains how to delete your **Nudge Drive** account (Android app by the Nudge Drive team) and what happens to your data.

**Your files are not deleted.** They stay in your own Google Drive or S3 storage. Deleting your account removes Nudge Drive's records about you and its access to your storage.

## Option 1: In the app

1. Open Nudge Drive and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm:
   - with your **password**, or
   - if you signed up with Google (or forgot your password), tap **Email me a code** and enter the 6-digit code we send to your account's email. The code expires in 15 minutes.
4. Tap **Delete**. You are signed out and the account is gone.

## Option 2: On the web

1. Go to [nudge.qd.je/delete-account](https://nudge.qd.je/delete-account).
2. Sign in with the account you want to delete.
3. Confirm with your password or with a code emailed to you, the same way as in the app.

## Option 3: By email

If you cannot sign in, email [support@nudge.qd.je](mailto:support@nudge.qd.je) **from the email address of your account** with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm the request. We will delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done.

## What is deleted

As soon as you confirm, we:

- **revoke Nudge Drive's access** to every Google account you connected;
- **permanently delete** from our server:
  - your account details (name, email, password hash, verification records);
  - your connected Google Drive and S3 accounts, including their encrypted access tokens and keys;
  - the list of your files and folders (names, sizes, types, dates and folder structure);
  - your share links (they stop working immediately) and invites you sent or received;
  - your activity history, sign-in sessions (including stored IP addresses and device names), API keys and upload rules;
  - cached thumbnails and document previews of your files.

We then send a short email confirming the deletion.

## What is kept

- **Your files** in Google Drive and S3 are untouched, including the folder Nudge Drive created in your Drive. You can manage or delete them directly in Google Drive or with your S3 provider.
- **Database backups:** our daily backups are kept for 14 days, so copies of your account data disappear completely within **14 days** of deletion. Backups are only used to recover from server failures.
- **Support emails** you sent us are kept for up to 2 years, unless you ask us to delete them too.
- **On your phone:** uninstall the app or clear its data to remove settings and cached files. Files you received with Nearby share stay in **Downloads › Nudge Drive** until you delete them.

## Delete some data without deleting your account

- **Files and folders:** delete them in the app. Deleted files go to the Trash first; deleting them forever from the Trash (or deleting a folder) also deletes them from your Google Drive or S3 storage.
- **Share links:** turn off a link from the file's details or **Shared links & invites**.
- **Storage accounts:** disconnect them in the **Drives** tab, and remove access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- **API keys:** revoke them under **API keys**.
- **Anything else:** email [support@nudge.qd.je](mailto:support@nudge.qd.je).
